# Drawing Volume Profiles

- Fixed Range Volume Profile: click and drag across the chart to define the start/end time and price range. The two blue handles can be dragged independently to adjust the range.
- Anchored Volume Profile: click one candle to set the anchor. The profile recalculates from that candle through the latest loaded candle and is rendered against the right edge of the chart. Drag the blue anchor handle to reposition it.
- Both profiles display a 32-row histogram, POC, and approximate 70% value-area high/low.

Volume limitation: candle OHLCV volume is distributed evenly across the price bins each candle spans. This is an estimate, not exchange/broker trade-level volume-at-price or bid/ask volume.
